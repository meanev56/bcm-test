Q6 — Add paragraphs, HR separators, toggle image paragraphs
Behaviour
Click original button → add copies of the three original paragraphs in randomised order, after the last paragraph.

Insert <hr> after every pair when another paragraph follows: P1, P2, HR, P3, P4, HR, P5…

Apply on initial load and after every click.

Second button (created via JS only) toggles paragraphs containing images. If hidden, newly added image paragraphs must stay hidden too.

Count all paragraphs for separator placement, including hidden ones.





| Scenario | Steps | Expected | Observed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Initial load | Open page | 3 paragraphs, HR after P2 (before P3) | ✅ P1, P2, HR, P3 |
| One click | Click "Hit me" | 6 paragraphs, HR after P2 and P4 | ✅ |
| Two clicks | Click twice | 9 paragraphs, HR after P2, P4, P6, P8 | ✅ |
| Random order | Click several times | Each batch of 3 paragraphs is shuffled | ✅ Verified in console by logging order |
| Toggle before adding | Click toggle | Image paragraph hidden; separators still count it | ✅ HR positions unchanged |
| Toggle after adding | Add, then toggle | All image paragraphs (old and new) hidden | ✅ |
| Toggle off | Click toggle again | All image paragraphs visible | ✅ |

Evidence: I ran this in Chrome DevTools, logging article.querySelectorAll('p').length and the positions of hr.sep after each action. The counts and HR positions matched the table above. I also confirmed keyboard focus reaches both buttons (the JS-created one is a real <button> and is focusable).
